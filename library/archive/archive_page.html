THE ARCHIVE

AN OFFICE OF FALLING PAPER

Somewhere in the infinite filing system of the internet is the page you asked for. It is not lost — it is shelved, which is worse. This is the archive's reading room: documents drift down forever, dust drifts up, and every so often your missing page tumbles past with a fragment of its own history written on it.

Tap a falling page to hold it still and read what it says. Then let it go. That's the whole room. Some people find it boring. Those people have never lost something to a filing system.
