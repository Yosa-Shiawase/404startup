YOSA-SHIAWASE

BUILDER OF PLAYABLE 404 PAGES

Every website has one page everyone visits and nobody remembers: the 404. The most common error on the web is also the most boring page ever shipped — a dead end, a shrug, a dinosaur at best.

I started 404STARTUP to fix that. The idea is simple: the internet dies, your 404 page shouldn't. Every world here is a fully playable, fully offline error page — hand-drawn canvas, synthesized sound, zero dependencies, one file. When a visitor's connection drops, instead of a dead end they find a desert, a radar console, a code stream, a high score.

The library is free for anyone to drop into their site. For brands and projects I build custom worlds — your mascot, your palette, your 404 — like the NIRAKSHAN fraud-radar console in the work section.

Twelve worlds are live. The shelf keeps growing.
