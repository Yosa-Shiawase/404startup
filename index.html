HOW TO GET ITOne file. Zero dependencies.

Copy 404.html into any site and most hosts pick it up automatically — GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, Render. Nginx: error_page 404 /404.html; · Apache: ErrorDocument 404 /404.html. Serve sw.js and register it once, and your 404 works even when the whole internet doesn't.
