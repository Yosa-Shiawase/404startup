HOW IT WORKSDead internet, live page

A service worker caches your chosen 404 the first time a visitor arrives. After that, the browser never gets to show its boring dinosaur again.

1 · VISIT ONCE, ONLINE

The 404 page quietly downloads into the visitor's browser.

2 · THE INTERNET DIES

Dead links, offline mode, expired connection — any navigation fails.

3 · THE PAGE TAKES OVER

Instead of a dead end: a themed world, a high score, a reason to stay.
